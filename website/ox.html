FAQ

What is OXPlayer?
OXPlayer is a personal media library app with a Netflix-style interface. You sync through our Telegram bot and stream videos you upload yourself we do not host or sell movies.
How do I connect Telegram?
Sync only with @OXPlayerBot. Open the bot in Telegram, sign in to the OXPlayer app with Telegram's official authorization, and your library stays linked to your account. We never ask for your Telegram password or access your private chats.
How do I add movies and videos?
Send a video file to @OXPlayerBot. After the bot finishes processing, the title appears in your OXPlayer library with posters and metadata.
Does OXPlayer provide movies or TV shows?
No. OXPlayer does not host, sell, or distribute content. It only organizes and plays videos that you personally send to the bot.
Which platforms are available?
Android is available now. iOS and Web are coming soon. Windows and Linux are on the roadmap.
Can I continue watching from where I left off?
Yes. Watch progress is saved to your account so you can resume where you stopped.
Are favorites and watchlists synced?
Yes. Favorites, watchlists, history, and playback progress stay tied to your OXPlayer account when you sign in again.
Is my content private?
Your library belongs to your account. Only media you send to @OXPlayerBot is indexed not your other Telegram messages or files.
How do I get help?
Message our support bot @MySupport2026Bot on Telegram for questions or issues.
